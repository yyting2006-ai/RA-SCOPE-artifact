# Data and licence boundary

## Included derived material

The artifact includes source item identifiers, original-work lineage identifiers, source-family labels, professional adjudicated grades, model probabilities, predictions, and aggregate statistics. The study's annotation and derived-result tables are supplied for scholarly verification of the reported experiments.

## Original content

- **StoryWeaver:** original pages remain on StoryWeaver. Users must follow the platform's open-content and item attribution terms: <https://storyweaver.org.in/open-content>.
- **Global African Storybook Project:** original stories remain in the first-party repository. Licences vary by item and include CC BY and CC BY-NC: <https://github.com/global-asp/global-asp>.
- **Global Digital Library:** original content and item metadata remain with the provider: <https://content.digitallibrary.io/api/>.
- **Chinese Graded Readers Standards for International Chinese Language Education:** the source standard is cited but not redistributed in this artifact.
- **Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for International Chinese Language Education (GF 0025--2021):** the official record is <https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/202111/t20211118_580755.html>.

No story text, page image, proprietary font, login token, or provider download archive is included. Reusers are responsible for checking the licence attached to each original item and preserving required attribution.
